- Early game power progression for me is: IE waterwheel so I can make Mekanism wind turbines. After that I go for Ethylene in a Gas Burning Generator until I can get an Advanced Generator going. Then I go straight for Nuclearcraft Fusion Reactor. RF Tool powercells give you access to wireless power until you can get Flux Networks going (that is a deep, deep rabbit hole).
- IC2
- A very helpful thing is the existence of mekanism and their universal-cables. They are by far not the best option for transferring rf around, but they can power your Industrialcraft machines by converting RF to EU.
- And they do it in way, that you don’t have to worry about transformer-upgrades and explosions. It is almost as comfortable to use them, like it is with RF-machines. Later on, when you have higher power-setups they can be your fastest machines around, just throw as much overclocker-upgrades in as you like and get happy.
- Early game power
- For the start you have different options for passive power-generators. A couple of mekanisms wind generators can do a pretty good job, also the thermoelectric generators from immersive engineering and the RTGs from Nuclearcraft, if you have access to some isotopes.
- I'm playing through E2:E at the moment and there are a few things I've discovered that I haven't really seen mentioned in other tips threads, so I figured I'd post everything I could think of here for others who may be interested.
- The Sleeping Bag from Openblocks will let you sleep anywhere without resetting your spawn point - combined with the Sleep charm this is an instant skip to daytime.
- Slimesling + Quantumsuit jetpack will get you around quite quickly, much quicker than either one alone.
- A tier 3 (Bound Leather + Iron Frame) large backpack from the Backpacks mod can go in its own slot outside of your inventory, has its own keybind and holds 54 items - I use it for tucking away all my "core" gear when I need inventory space for a big craft job, or if I've been treasure hunting and need more room
- The Storage Bag from Cyclic is another 77 slots and has auto-pickup and auto-deposit features, as well as being able to change the bag's color on the fly. No keybinding, but a direct upgrade to the Satchel until you can get it upgraded and a good Holding enchant.
- The Diamond backpack from Iron Backpacks will give you 77 slots, I'm using mine to store all my various tools, some food, things like buckets and tanks, Botania rods, blood orb, etc. - can be keybound!
- Once you can make steel casing, you can add a Storage Scanner which will let you view and craft from any nearby inventories - RFTools or not. This has been huge for me in being able to quickly craft everything I need, basically a poor man's AE2. It does have some issues with crafting certain things with durability, like IC2 batteries - just pull out the materials and craft elsewhere.
- Storage Boxes from IC2 work the exact same way but are easier to make and can be made at higher storage tiers - this is a great alternative and upgrade to the previous.
- Twilight Forest is easy to get to and has massive trees to veinmine if you need a lot of wood without a tree farm.
- Use the Smart I/O on your tinker's smeltery - makes plates/gears/etc really easy to make.
- Make sure to fill your tinker's stuff with pyrotheum (from the Nether) when you can, it's a good speed upgrade and they don't even consume that much of it.
- Concrete Floors in your base = zoom zoom.
- Get a bucket and a vanilla cow to always top-off your dairy level, you can drink milk any time so you can walk up to a cow with a bucket and literally top off your dairy in seconds. Keep one at your base and just make top tier food without dairy so you can skip all the animania stuff.

### Power Generation & Management
- **Early Game Progression:** Start with IE waterwheels to craft Mekanism wind turbines. Move to Ethylene in a Gas Burning Generator, then Advanced Generators, and finally a Nuclearcraft Fusion Reactor.
- **Passive Power:** Early on, use Mekanism wind generators, IE thermoelectric generators, or Nuclearcraft RTGs (if isotopes are available).
- **IC2 Integration:** Use Mekanism universal-cables to power Industrialcraft machines. They convert RF to EU automatically, preventing explosions and removing the need for transformer upgrades.
- **Wireless Power:** Use RFTools powercells for wireless power until you can progress to Flux Networks.

### Storage & Inventory
- **Backpacks:**
- **Backpacks Mod:** Tier 3 Large Backpack (54 slots) fits in its own slot and has a keybind.
- **Cyclic:** Storage Bag (77 slots) features auto-pickup and auto-deposit.
- **Iron Backpacks:** Diamond backpack (77 slots) is great for tools and can be keybound.
- **Storage Systems:**
- **RFTools Storage Scanner:** Acts as a "poor man's AE2," allowing you to view and craft from all nearby inventories once you have steel casings.
- **IC2 Storage Boxes:** A great alternative or upgrade to the Storage Scanner for higher-tier storage.

### Movement & Exploration
- **Fast Travel:** Combine the Slimesling with a Quantumsuit jetpack for extremely high-speed travel.
- **Base Speed:** Use Concrete Floors in your base to increase movement speed ("zoom zoom").
- **Skipping Night:** Use the Openblocks Sleeping Bag combined with a Sleep charm to instantly skip to daytime without resetting your spawn point.
- **Twilight Forest:** Visit this dimension to veinmine massive trees if you need large quantities of wood quickly.

### Crafting & Tools
- **Tinker's Construct:**
- Use **Smart I/O** on the smeltery to automate the creation of plates and gears.
- Use **Pyrotheum** as fuel in the smeltery for a significant speed upgrade.
- **Automated Crafting:** The RFTools Storage Scanner allows for quick crafting using materials from nearby chests, though it may struggle with items that have durability (like IC2 batteries).

### Resources & Survival
- **Dairy/Nutrition:** Keep a vanilla cow and a bucket at your base. Drinking milk allows you to top off your dairy nutrition level in seconds, allowing you to skip complex Animania food processing.
