## ANSWER GUIDE FRAMEWORK

_(Note: Full answers provided below)_

### Answers and Explanations

**Question 1: B** - This is the definition of scarcity.

**Question 2: B** - Opportunity cost is what you give up to do something else. The trade-off between studying subjects exemplifies both concepts.

**Question 5:**

- Emma's opportunity cost of 1 essay = 3 problem sets (15÷5)
- James's opportunity cost of 1 essay = 1.5 problem sets (12÷8)
- James has comparative advantage (lower OC)
- The trade is beneficial: Emma gives up less in essays than she'd need to produce in problem sets

**Question 11:**

- a) Canada: absolute advantage in oil; Mexico: wheat
- b) Canada: 0.25 tonnes wheat; Mexico: 0.5 tonnes wheat
- c) Canada: comparative advantage in oil; Mexico: wheat
- d) Yes - need to check if trade price (30 oil for 60 wheat = 2 wheat per oil) is within both countries' opportunity costs

**Question 23:**

- a) Year 1: (100×$2) + (50×$4) = $400 billion Year 2: (120×$2.50) + (60×$5) = $600 billion
- b) Real GDP Year 2: (120×$2) + (60×$4) = $480 billion
- c) GDP Deflator: ($600/$480) × 100 = 125
- d) Inflation rate: (125-100)/100 × 100 = 25%

**Question 29:**

- a) Labour force = 400 + 20 = 420 million
- b) Unemployment rate = (20/420) × 100 = 4.76%
- c) LFPR = (420/500) × 100 = 84%
- d) Employment ratio = (400/500) × 100 = 80%

**Question 36:** 70 ÷ 2.5 = 28 years approximately

**Question 49:**

- a) AE = $100 + 0.80(Y - $200) + $150 + $200 + $50 = $430 + 0.80Y
- b) Y = $430 + 0.80Y → 0.20Y = $430 → Y = $2,150
- c) Multiplier = 1/(1-0.80) = 5
- d) New AE = $480 + 0.80Y → Y = $2,400

**Question 63:**

- a) Multiplier = 1/0.10 = 10
- b) $5,000 × 10 = $50,000 billion increase
- c) All deposits go to banks; banks loan all excess reserves; no currency withdrawals

**Question 77:**

- a) Multiplier = 1/(1-0.75) = 4
- b) $50B × 4 = $200 billion increase in GDP
- c) Tax multiplier = -0.75/(1-0.75) = -3; $50B × 3 = $150 billion increase
