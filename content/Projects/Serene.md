- Daily mood checkins
- Daily affirmations
- journalling
- mood trend and stress logging
- finding therapists based on user location (google maps?)
- self-care streak

**1. Maya, 19 — First-year university student**

- **Challenges:** anxiety about academic performance, social isolation, imposter syndrome.
    
- **Behaviors:** studies late, avoids social events, checks grades obsessively.
    
- **Needs:** reassurance, structure, small social exposure goals, reminders to rest.
    
- **Effective features:** mood journaling, gentle reminders, pet-based encouragement.
    

  

**2. Leo, 22 — Final-year engineering student**

- **Challenges:** burnout, sleep deprivation, perfectionism.
    
- **Behaviors:** overworks on projects, skips meals, uses caffeine excessively.
    
- **Needs:** balanced schedule, stress tracking, relaxation prompts.
    
- **Effective features:** reflection tasks, rest validation, data on sleep impact.
    

  

**3. Sara, 21 — Art major living off-campus**

- **Challenges:** depression, low motivation, poor routine.
    
- **Behaviors:** isolates, inconsistent sleep, stops creative work.
    
- **Needs:** gentle activation, journaling, progress visualization to rebuild consistency.
    
- **Effective features:** non-judgmental prompts, flexible habit system.
    

  

**4. Amir, 20 — International student**
