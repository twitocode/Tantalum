Multipliers (focusing on expansionary policies) 
- gov spending multiplier $\frac{1}{1-MPC}$
	- $\uparrow G$
 - tax multiplier
	 - increase AD means $\downarrow T$
	 - $\frac{-MPC}{1-MPC}$, negative sign. when taxes decreases, households spend most but save some. multiplier is close to 1 but not 1.
- balanced budget multiplier
	- $\Delta G, \Delta T$ increase spending but dont increase debt? theyre both $10B, the AD curve will shift over 10 billion dollars with a multiplier of 1
	- $\frac{1}{1-MPC} + \frac{-MPC}{1-MPC} = \frac{1-MPC}{1-MPC} = 1$


Automatic Stabilizers
- no legislation required needed (like EI, welfare)
	- before the great depression people thought things would fix themselves
- lessen the severity of swings around LR equilibirum (real gdp)
- makes the peaks and troughs shallowere in stylized business cycle diagram

Lags and coordinations
- types of lags
	- fiscal -> pass legislation
	- monetary -> much quicker
		- $\downarrow\text{interest rates}$ and takes time for firms to respond to the new interest rate
		- firms dont make factories in 1 week. it'll take time for the interest rate changes to change the firms' behaviours
- policy coordination
	- dont fight each other.
		- goverment doing something, and BOC doing something, its a waste of resources
		- *just make sure everyone is on the same page*
